Colour-blind safety: lanes never rely on hue alone. Every bar starts with a 14 dp glyph, lanes keep a fixed vertical order (overlay · main · looks · stickers · effects · text · audio), and the palette splits into a dark group (teal, violet, blue, plum: white labels) and a light group (amber, slate, orange, lime: Ink0 labels).
