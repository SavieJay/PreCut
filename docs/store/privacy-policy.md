# PreCut privacy policy

**Effective:** 20 October 2026

PreCut is a video editor for Android. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short, PreCut doesn't collect, send or share any of it.

## What PreCut collects
Nothing. PreCut has no internet access: the app doesn't ask Android for permission to use the network. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your videos, photos, recordings and projects never leave your phone through PreCut.

## What stays on your phone
To edit, PreCut works with files on your device:

- **Photos and videos you pick.** You choose them with Android's picker, and PreCut can read only those. If the picker can't keep access to a file, PreCut saves a copy in its own storage so your project still works later.
- **Microphone.** PreCut asks for the microphone only when you record a voiceover. The recording is saved in the app's storage as part of your project.
- **Your projects.** PreCut saves drafts, covers, preview copies and other working files in its own storage on your phone.
- **Exports.** Videos, GIFs, audio and photos you export are saved to your phone's gallery or music folders, where other apps can see them like any other media.
- **Notifications.** PreCut shows a notification while an export runs, so you can follow or cancel it.

When you share an export, Android's share sheet sends it to the app you choose. What happens next is up to that app.

## Text to speech
If you use text to speech, PreCut passes your text to the text-to-speech engine installed on your phone. That engine's own privacy policy applies to the text.

## Deleting your data
Deleting a project removes its files from the app. Settings → Clear preview cache frees working copies. Uninstalling PreCut removes everything it stored, except media you exported to your gallery.

## Children
PreCut doesn't collect information from anyone, including children.
