## Short description (80)

## Full description (4000)

PreCut is a video editor for your phone. Put clips on a timeline, cut and arrange them, add music, text, stickers and effects, and export in up to 4K. No watermark, no ads, no account, and nothing leaves your phone.

EDIT ON A TIMELINE
• Trim, split, duplicate, reorder and replace clips with snapping to edges, beats and the playhead
• Speed from 0.1x to 100x, with speed curves for smooth ramps
• Reverse clips and freeze frames
• Crop, rotate, mirror and set the aspect ratio for TikTok, Reels, Shorts or YouTube
• Undo and redo every change; drafts save as you go

LOOK AND FEEL
• 61 filters (life, portrait, film, movie, night and more), plus your own .cube LUTs
• Adjust brightness, contrast, saturation, HSL and curves
• 46 effects: party, glitch, retro, light leaks, art, snow and rain, lens, split screens and more
• Motion blur for fast movement
• Keyframes with easing curves for movement, opacity, masks and colour
• 30 transitions between clips: dissolve, slide, push, wipe, circle, clock, ink, zoom, spin, glitch and more
• In, out and combo animations

LAYERS
• Picture-in-picture overlays with blend modes (multiply, screen, overlay and more)
• Masks: linear, mirror, circle, rectangle, heart and star, with feathering
• Chroma key to remove a green or blue screen
• Canvas colour, blur or picture behind your video

TEXT AND STICKERS
• Styled text in 24 bundled fonts or your own: outline, shadow, glow, background, curved text and animations
• 35 one-tap text templates for titles, captions and social posts
• Text to speech
• Emoji, animated and 3D sticker packs, animated GIF and WebP stickers, your own images, and drawing

AUDIO
• Several music and sound tracks, each with its own volume, fades and speed
• Record voiceovers, with voice effects
• Extract the sound from a video
• Mark beats to cut on the rhythm

EXPORT
• 480p to 4K, 24 to 60 fps, H.264 or HEVC, and HDR where your phone supports it
• Export keeps running in the background, with progress in a notification
• Export as a GIF, as audio only, or save a single frame as a photo

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
PreCut has no internet access. Your videos, recordings and projects stay on your phone.
